Delete your Rhythm & Flow account

For the Rhythm & Flow app and website.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open the Rhythm & Flow app (or the web app) and sign in.
  2. Go to You, then Settings.
  3. Choose Delete my account and type your password to confirm.

If you have a plan, we stop it with PayFast first, so you are not charged again.

Can't get into the app? Ask us to do it

Email info@rhythmandflow.co.za from the email address on your account, with the subject "Please delete my account". We will confirm it by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account details: name, username, email address and password.
  • Your journal, practice progress, bookings, account credit and notification addresses.

What we keep

  • Records of payments you made, because we must keep financial records. They are kept without your name or email address.

More detail is in our privacy policy and our policies.
