Health and wellness information

Rhythm & Flow shares general wellness content: movement, breathing, meditation, journalling and class information. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor, physiotherapist or other health professional. Check with a health professional before starting a new kind of exercise, particularly if you are pregnant, injured, or have a health condition. Move within your own limits and stop if something hurts or you feel unwell.
