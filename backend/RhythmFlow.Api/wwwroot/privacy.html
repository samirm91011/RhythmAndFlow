Privacy policy

Rhythm & Flow app and website. Last updated 29 October 2026.

Rhythm & Flow ("we", "us") is a wellness studio in South Africa. This page explains what personal information the Rhythm & Flow app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, username, email address and a password. The password is stored in a scrambled form, so nobody can read it.
  • Your practice: which practices you watch, how far you get, the practices you save, and your intentions and preferences.
  • Your journal: check-ins about how you feel, reflections and gratitude notes you choose to write. These belong to you and are not shown to other customers.
  • Bookings: the classes and events you book or buy tickets for, and any account credit you hold.
  • Payments: when you pay, you do so on PayFast's own secure page. We never see or store your card details. PayFast tells us whether a payment succeeded, the amount and a payment reference.
  • Your phone: if you allow notifications, a notification address for your device so we can send class reminders. We also keep technical error records to fix problems.

Why we use it

  • To create and run your account, and give you the practices and plan you chose.
  • To take and confirm payments, send receipts, and manage cancellations and refunds.
  • To manage class and event bookings and send reminders.
  • To suggest practices that suit how you say you feel, and to show you your own progress.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

Who we share it with

We only share what is needed, with the services that help us run the app:

  • PayFast takes payments for us.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts the app's servers and stores uploaded videos, in South Africa (Cape Town).
  • An email service sends the emails you ask for, such as sign-up codes, receipts and booking confirmations.
  • Google (Firebase) delivers notifications to your phone.
  • YouTube plays our free practice videos. YouTube may collect information as described in Google's own privacy policy.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your journal, progress, bookings, credit, notification addresses and reminders. Payment records are kept without your name or email, because we must keep financial records.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and change your details in the app under You, then Settings, then Account.
  • You can download a copy of everything we hold about you in the app under You, then Settings, then Download my data.
  • You can delete your account in the app under You, then Settings, then Delete my account. See also how to delete your account.
  • You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it or delete it, by emailing us.
  • You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Memberships, bookings and payments

How subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, refunds, event bookings and online or technical problems are handled is set out in our policies. Payments are taken securely by PayFast, so we never see or keep your card details.

Health and wellness information

Mood check-ins, reflections and gratitude notes are personal wellbeing notes that you choose to write. We use them only to show you your own journal and your own feel-better summary, and to suggest a practice. They are not medical records, and Rhythm & Flow does not give medical advice. See the health and wellness note.

Children

The app is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
